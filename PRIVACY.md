# Fitness Tracker: privacy policy

_Last updated: 14 November 2026_

Fitness Tracker is a personal diet, walking, workout, sleep and water tracker for Android, made by Yash Gumaste.

## What the app stores, and where
- Everything you log (meals, water, walks, recorded routes, workouts, weight, sleep, how you feel, your profile and goals) is stored **on your phone**. There is no Fitness Tracker server and no account.
- Android's own backup may copy the app's data to your Google account, if you have that turned on for your phone.

## Google Drive backup (optional)
- If you turn on **Back up to Google Drive**, the app saves a backup file of your data in the hidden **app data folder** of **your own** Google Drive (`drive.appdata` access).
- The app can only see the backup files it created there. It cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.
- You can delete the backups at any time from Google Drive (Storage → Backups) or turn the backup off in the app.

## AI features
- To work out a meal's calories and macros, or for coach tips, the text you type and a summary of your day are sent to the AI service you choose: **Google Gemini** (the free default) or **Anthropic Claude** (with your own key). Google may use what's sent on the free tier to improve its products. Nothing else is sent.

## Health Connect
- Only if you connect it, the app reads your step count and writes your meals, water, walks (with routes for walks you record), workouts, weight and sleep to Health Connect, for the record types you allow.

## Location
- Your location is used **only while you're recording a walk** (Start → Finish), to draw the route. It's stored on your phone (and in Health Connect / your Drive backup if you use them), never sent anywhere else. The map pictures come from OpenStreetMap, which sees the map area being viewed, not your account.
